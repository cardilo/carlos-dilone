About

Where strategy
meets partnership

I've spent 13+ years at the intersection of commercial rigour and human relationships — from manufacturing floors in Belgium and Sweden to the boardrooms of Canada's largest institutions.

Today I lead strategic sourcing at TD Bank, managing a $100M+ CAD annual spend portfolio in one of the country's most regulated environments. Previously at Indigo, I built a vendor platform that served 3,000+ suppliers and delivered $2.6M in savings within eight months.

I speak at procurement summits, mentor professionals entering the Canadian market, and believe that the best commercial outcomes are built on trust as much as terms.
